Privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]. Neighborly is operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [ADDRESS] (“we”). This policy explains what personal data we process under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Your email address or mobile number, used only to sign you in. It is never shown to other members.
  • Your display name, optional bio, and the flat or room number you give when joining a circle.
  • Items you list and their photos. We remove location data (EXIF/GPS) from photos before upload.
  • Loan records, chat messages, condition photos taken at handover, ratings and trust-score history.

Who can see it

  • Members of your circle see your name, trust score and listings.
  • Your flat number is visible only to circle admins and moderators, and to a neighbour after you approve their request or they approve yours.
  • Chat and condition photos are visible only to the two people in a loan, and to circle moderators if a dispute is raised.

Why we use it

To run the lending service you asked for, keep members safe, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse. We do not sell data or show ads.

Where it is stored

With our processor Supabase, in the [Mumbai (ap-south-1)] region. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Condition photos from completed loans are kept for [12 months] as evidence for the other party, without your name attached. Read notifications are removed after 90 days.

Your rights

You can access and correct your profile in the app, and delete your account from the profile page at any time. For other requests or grievances, contact our Grievance Officer: [NAME], [EMAIL]. We respond within [30] days.
